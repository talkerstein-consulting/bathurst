# Digital Presence: Business Owner Questions for Canadian SMEs

53 questions Canadian (especially Toronto and Ontario) business owners ask about websites, e-commerce, user experience and custom development, grouped by buying stage: Awareness, Consideration, Decision and Implementation. Questions are written the way owners phrase them; they are representative examples, not measured search volumes. Answer directions are **Recommendations**. Detail and sources are in [business-websites.md](business-websites.md), [ecommerce.md](ecommerce.md), [conversion-and-user-experience.md](conversion-and-user-experience.md) and [custom-development.md](custom-development.md). Accessibility law questions belong in [../06-canadian-regulations/digital-accessibility.md](../06-canadian-regulations/digital-accessibility.md).

## Awareness questions about websites and e-commerce

### 1. Why isn't my website bringing in any calls or enquiries?

- **Underlying problem:** Site exists but produces no leads
- **Search intent:** Informational
- **Relevant industry:** All local services
- **Answer direction:** Check findability, mobile, speed and calls to action; see lead-generation.md
- **Tools or solutions:** Search Console, analytics, call tracking
- **Talkerstein service:** Web design, conversion audit
- **Supporting sources:** [CFIB 2025](https://www.cfib-fcei.ca/en/research-economic-analysis/sme-digital-presence), [Google: page experience](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/page-experience)

### 2. Is my website too slow, and does it actually matter?

- **Underlying problem:** Visitors leaving before the page loads
- **Search intent:** Informational
- **Relevant industry:** All
- **Answer direction:** Explain LCP, INP and CLS targets; speed affects customers more than rank
- **Tools or solutions:** PageSpeed Insights, Search Console
- **Talkerstein service:** Performance optimization
- **Supporting sources:** [web.dev Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals), [Google: Core Web Vitals](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-web-vitals)

### 3. What are Core Web Vitals and do I need to care about them?

- **Underlying problem:** Jargon in SEO reports

- **Relevant industry:** All
- **Answer direction:** Three metrics, good thresholds, 75th percentile; recommended by Google but no ranking guarantee
