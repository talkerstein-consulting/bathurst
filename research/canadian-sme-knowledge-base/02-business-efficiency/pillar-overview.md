## How much time Canadian small businesses lose to admin and regulation

CFIB's *Canada's Red Tape Report* (news release, January 27, 2025) is the main Canadian measure of administrative burden ([CFIB: Small businesses spend over 250 hours a year wrapped up in red tape](https://www.cfib-fcei.ca/en/media/small-businesses-spend-over-250-hours-or-32-business-days-a-year-wrapped-up-in-red-tape)):

- Businesses spent **735 hours** on regulatory compliance in 2024, of which **256 hours (about 32 business days)** was red tape, up from 189 hours in 2020.
- Total regulatory cost was about **$51.5 billion** in 2024; CFIB attributes **$17.9 billion** to red tape.
- Firms with fewer than five employees spent **$10,208 per employee** on compliance, versus $1,374 for firms with 100+ employees.
- 87% of owners said excessive regulation significantly reduces productivity and growth.

These are CFIB's estimates based on its own member survey method, not a Statistics Canada measurement. Red tape itself is a government problem, but the tasks around it (filing, record-keeping, forms, payroll, GST/HST) are where software and process changes can save owners time. Filing rules are in [../06-canadian-regulations/federal-requirements.md](../06-canadian-regulations/federal-requirements.md).

BDC's operational efficiency team reports that only 15 to 20% of a typical employee's workday goes to purely productive activities ([BDC: How to combine continuous improvement and technology](https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/operations/operational-efficiency/how-combine-continuous-improvement-technology), undated). Treat this as BDC's consulting observation, not survey data.

| SMEs using generative AI | 30% | Same |

BDC's chief message is that the gap is execution, not adoption: almost every SME buys technology, but outcomes are uneven. BDC models up to 38% higher SME productivity if all firms reached top-tier digital maturity (an **Estimate** from a non-probability survey of 1,500 owners, February 2026).

Note: Statistics Canada's Q3 2026 release says it is the **final** iteration of the Canadian Survey on Business Conditions, so later quarterly AI-use figures from that series will not exist.

## Main efficiency problems Canadian SMEs face

From the sources in this folder (details and links in each file):

1. **Admin and compliance time:** red tape alone is about 32 business days a year (CFIB, 2025).
2. **Cost pressure:** 64.3% of businesses expected cost-related obstacles in Q2 2026 and 59.8% in Q3 2026 (StatCan CSBC, links above); recruiting skilled employees was expected by 25.2% in Q3 2026. Saving staff time is one of the few controllable levers.
3. **Disconnected tools:** BDC warns against using a CRM "in a silo" and advises defining integrations before buying ([BDC: CRM system](https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/marketing-sales-export/sales/how-use-technology-boost-sales)).
4. **Automating broken processes:** BDC warns that copying current processes onto machines risks "automating waste" ([BDC, 2025-02-12](https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/operations/automation-robotics/how-to-modify-your-processes-for-an-automation-project)).
5. **Low AI relevance perceived:** 79.1% of businesses without AI plans say AI is not relevant to their goods or services (StatCan CSBC Q3 2026).

## Canada Digital Adoption Program (CDAP) status, checked 2026-11-12

**CDAP is closed to new applicants.** Do not tell owners they can apply.

- **Boost Your Business Technology** ($15,000 grant for a digital adoption plan, plus a 0% BDC loan up to $100,000): closed to new applications on **February 19, 2024**, per BDC's CDAP page ([BDC: Canada Digital Adoption Program loan](https://www.bdc.ca/en/financing/business-loans/canada-digital-adoption-program), checked 2026-11-12). Businesses with a valid grant agreement signed before that date may still be eligible for the loan if they meet program deadlines; BDC says extensions are not permitted. BDC points to its LIFT offer as an alternative.
- **Grow Your Business Online** ($2,400 micro-grant): closed to new applications on **September 30, 2024**, per a grant aggregator ([GrantCompass CDAP page](https://grantcompass.ca/canada-digital-adoption-program), updated September/October 2026). This date was not confirmed on a government page; the former ISED CDAP page now redirects to the ISED home page (checked 2026-11-12). Treat the exact date as **Unverified**.
- Context: The Logic reported in February 2024 that the $4 billion program had issued $274 million in grants and $280 million in loans when the $15,000 stream stopped taking applications ([The Logic, 2024-02-21](https://thelogic.co/briefing/ottawa-closes-key-parts-of-4b-canada-digital-adoption-program-leaving-billions-unused/)).

**Recommendation:** Check BDC and ISED directly before mentioning any replacement funding. No direct federal replacement was confirmed in this research.

## Common misconceptions about business efficiency

- **"Buying software fixes the problem."** BDC: 96% of SMEs invest in digital tools but outcomes are uneven (BDC 2026). Process first, tool second.
- **"Automation is for big companies."** BDC lists this among myths holding firms back (BDC 2026). Free tiers exist (see [business-automation.md](business-automation.md)).
- **"A CRM is a contact list."** BDC says the benefit comes from the integrated plan, not the software ([BDC: CRM system](https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/marketing-sales-export/sales/how-use-technology-boost-sales)).
- **"CDAP will pay for it."** Closed (see above).
