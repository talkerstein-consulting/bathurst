# Business Owner Questions: Running a More Efficient Business (Canada)

56 questions Canadian small business owners ask about admin, automation, CRMs and operations, grouped by buying stage: Awareness, Consideration, Decision and Implementation. Each lists the underlying problem, search intent, industry, a recommended answer direction, tools, the related Talkerstein service and sources.

Questions are written in owners' words by the research team; they are **not** measured search queries, and no search volumes are claimed. Answer directions are **Recommendations** based on the linked sources. Prices were read on 2026-11-12. Detail is in [pillar-overview.md](pillar-overview.md), [business-automation.md](business-automation.md), [crm-and-workflows.md](crm-and-workflows.md) and [operational-efficiency.md](operational-efficiency.md).

## Awareness questions 1 to 5: owner recognizes the problem

### I spend my evenings doing paperwork. Is that normal for a small business?

- **Underlying problem:** Admin crowding out paid work
- **Search intent:** Informational
- **Industry:** All
- **Answer direction:** It is common: CFIB measured 256 hours of red tape per business in 2024. Separate unavoidable compliance from admin that can be automated.
- **Tools:** Accounting software, CRM
- **Talkerstein service:** Business consulting
- **Sources:** [CFIB red tape release, 2025](https://www.cfib-fcei.ca/en/media/small-businesses-spend-over-250-hours-or-32-business-days-a-year-wrapped-up-in-red-tape)

### Why does my team always seem busy but nothing gets finished?

- **Underlying problem:** Low productive time, unclear priorities
- **Search intent:** Informational
- **Industry:** Professional services, agencies
- **Answer direction:** BDC's consultants find only 15 to 20% of a typical workday is purely productive. Map one process and look for waiting, rework and interruptions.
- **Tools:** Task or project tool
- **Talkerstein service:** Business consulting
- **Sources:** [BDC: continuous improvement and technology](https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/operations/operational-efficiency/how-combine-continuous-improvement-technology)

### How do I know which tasks in my business are worth automating?

- **Underlying problem:** No inventory of repetitive work
- **Search intent:** Informational
- **Industry:** All
- **Answer direction:** List tasks done weekly that follow the same steps; automate only those that add customer value after waste is removed.
- **Tools:** Zapier, native integrations
- **Talkerstein service:** Automation
- **Sources:** [BDC: processes for automation, 2025](https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/operations/automation-robotics/how-to-modify-your-processes-for-an-automation-project)

### We keep losing track of leads. Do I need a CRM?

- **Underlying problem:** Leads in inboxes and spreadsheets
- **Search intent:** Problem-aware
- **Industry:** Trades, B2B, services
- **Answer direction:** If nobody can say who followed up with whom, a CRM is the fix; BDC frames it as strategy plus software.
- **Tools:** HubSpot free, Bigin, Jobber
- **Talkerstein service:** Automation (CRM setup)
- **Sources:** [BDC: free and low-cost CRMs](https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/technology/free-low-cost-applications/crm-solutions-free-low-cost-options)

### Customers keep calling just to book or reschedule. Is there a better way?

- **Underlying problem:** Phone tag for appointments
- **Search intent:** Problem-aware
- **Industry:** Clinics, home services, salons
- **Answer direction:** Online booking with automatic confirmations and reminders.
- **Tools:** Jobber, HubSpot meeting link
- **Talkerstein service:** Automation
- **Sources:** [Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/); [HubSpot CRM pricing](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/crm)
