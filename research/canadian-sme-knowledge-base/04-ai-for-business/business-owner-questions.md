# Business Owner Questions About Using AI

This is a database of 56 natural questions Canadian, Ontario and Toronto business owners ask about AI, grouped by buying stage: Awareness, Consideration, Decision and Implementation. Each row gives the underlying problem, search intent, typical industry, the answer direction, tools, the related Talkerstein service and supporting sources.

How to read it:

- **Questions are written as owners phrase them.** They are realistic examples, **not** measured search queries. No search volumes are claimed.
- **Answer directions are recommendations**, backed by the linked KB files and sources. Detailed answers live in [practical-ai-applications.md](practical-ai-applications.md), [ai-automation.md](ai-automation.md), [ai-agents-and-integrations.md](ai-agents-and-integrations.md) and [ai-privacy-and-governance.md](ai-privacy-and-governance.md).
- **Intent codes:** Info (learning), Comm (comparing options), Trans (ready to buy or hire), Nav (looking for a specific tool or page).
- Prices and legal status are as of 2026-11-12.
