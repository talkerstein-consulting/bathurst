# Business Owner Questions About Getting More Customers

This is a database of 52 natural questions Canadian, Ontario and Toronto business owners ask about winning customers, grouped by buying stage: Awareness, Consideration, Decision and Implementation. Each row gives the underlying problem, search intent, typical industry, the answer direction, tools, the related Talkerstein service and supporting sources.

How to read it:

- **Questions are written as owners phrase them.** They are realistic examples, **not** measured search queries. No search volumes are claimed.
- **Answer directions are recommendations**, backed by the linked KB files and sources. Detailed answers live in [customer-acquisition.md](customer-acquisition.md), [lead-generation.md](lead-generation.md), [marketing-and-advertising.md](marketing-and-advertising.md) and [seo-and-local-search.md](seo-and-local-search.md).
- **Intent codes:** Info (learning), Comm (comparing options), Trans (ready to buy or hire), Nav (looking for a specific tool or page).

## Patterns in these questions

**Opinion**, based on the questions above and the linked research:

- **Measurement comes up at every stage** (questions 1, 8, 30, 51). Owners cannot tell what works, which links to BDC's finding that a third of businesses struggle to measure ad return (see [customer-acquisition.md](customer-acquisition.md)).
- **Google Business Profile drives many practical questions** (3, 10, 13, 18, 40 to 43, 52). It is the cheapest high-impact fix for most local firms.
- **Trust and vendor risk** (29, 36) matter in a climate where weak demand and costs dominate ([CFIB Barometer](https://www.cfib-fcei.ca/en/research/business-barometer)).
