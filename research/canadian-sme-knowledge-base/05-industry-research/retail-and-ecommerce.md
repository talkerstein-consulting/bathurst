# Retail and eCommerce in Ontario: Growth, Operations, Digital and AI

This file covers independent shops, multi-location retailers and online (direct-to-consumer) brands in Toronto and Ontario. Wholesale and B2B ordering are in [manufacturing.md](manufacturing.md). Platform choice detail is in [ecommerce.md](../03-digital-presence/ecommerce.md) (planned) and [ecommerce-and-payments.md](../08-software-directory/ecommerce-and-payments.md) (planned).

## Retail and e-commerce sales in Canada and Ontario (December 2025)

From Statistics Canada's [Retail trade, December 2025](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260220/dq260220a-eng.htm) (published 2026-02-20):

- Seasonally adjusted retail e-commerce sales rose 3.6% to about $4.3 billion, or **6.1% of total retail trade** (5.8% in November).
- Ontario retail sales fell 0.2% to $25.9 billion, mainly from lower receipts at motor vehicle and parts dealers. Retail sales in the Toronto census metropolitan area rose 0.5%.

StatCan's e-commerce share counts retailers' online sales only. Market-research forecasts that include marketplaces, travel and digital goods are much higher and not comparable.

**Implication (opinion):** most retail spending is still in stores, so for most independents the website's job is to drive store visits and capture online orders, not replace the store.

## Business models in retail and eCommerce

| Model | Notes |
|---|---|
| Single-location independent | Local foot traffic; website and Google Business Profile drive visits |
| Omnichannel (store + online) | Buy online, pick up in store; shared inventory |
| DTC online brand | Ships across Canada; paid social and email driven |
| Marketplace seller | Amazon.ca and others; margin and fee pressure |
| Subscription | Recurring boxes or refills |

## Customer acquisition challenges for retailers

**Opinion, based on common practice; not measured:**

- Rising paid social costs for DTC brands; dependence on one channel.
- Competing on price with large retailers and marketplaces.
- Shipping cost and delivery-time expectations across a large country.
- Store inventory not visible online, so local searchers go elsewhere.

Consumer discovery data (friends and family, search, social, reviews) is in [customer-acquisition.md](../01-getting-more-customers/customer-acquisition.md). Price display rules (drip pricing) and review rules are covered there and in [federal-requirements.md](../06-canadian-regulations/federal-requirements.md).

## Operational bottlenecks in retail

**Opinion:** inventory mismatch between POS and website; manual product entry and photography; returns handling; staff scheduling around Ontario minimum wage changes each October 1 (see [ontario-requirements.md](../06-canadian-regulations/ontario-requirements.md)); GST/HST collection across provinces for online sales (see [federal-requirements.md](../06-canadian-regulations/federal-requirements.md)).

## Digital presence and website features for retail and eCommerce

| Feature | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Live stock by store, buy online pick up in store | Converts local searchers |
| Filters on every product grid | Findability (recommendation) |
| Clear all-in price, shipping cost and delivery times before checkout | Avoids drip-pricing problems |
| Returns and exchange policy page | Trust |
| Bilingual product data where selling into Quebec | Quebec has its own French-language rules (**Unverified:** not reviewed for this file) |
| Reviews, loyalty and email/SMS sign-up with CASL consent | Repeat purchase |
| Accessible checkout | AODA, see [digital-accessibility.md](../06-canadian-regulations/digital-accessibility.md) |

## Automation and AI use cases in retail

StatCan's Q3 2025 survey found only 5.7% of retail businesses planned to use AI to produce goods or deliver services in the next 12 months ([expected use of AI, Q3 2025](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/11-621-m/11-621-m2025011-eng.htm)). Across all AI-using businesses, common uses were text analytics (35.7%), data analytics (26.4%), chatbots (24.8%) and marketing automation (23.1%) ([AI use, Q2 2025](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/11-621-m/11-621-m2025008-eng.htm)).

**Automations (recommendations):** abandoned-cart and post-purchase email flows; low-stock alerts and purchase orders; syncing POS and online inventory; review requests after delivery.

**AI use cases (recommendations):** product descriptions and alt text from supplier data; customer service assistant for order status and returns; demand forecasting for reorders; segmenting customers for email.

## Common software and custom development for retail

**Examples (not an endorsement; usage share not verified):** Shopify and Shopify POS, Lightspeed, Square, WooCommerce; Klaviyo or Mailchimp for email; Canada Post and courier integrations.

**Custom development opportunities (recommendation; platform apps first):** store-level inventory and pickup integrations; product configurators or bundle builders; loyalty integrations between POS and site; headless storefronts only where the brand needs performance or design beyond themes.
