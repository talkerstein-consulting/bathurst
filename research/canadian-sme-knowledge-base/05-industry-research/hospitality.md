# Restaurants, Bars and Hospitality in Toronto: Growth, Operations, Digital and AI

This file covers restaurants, cafés, bars, caterers, small hotels and event venues in Toronto and Ontario. Employment rules (minimum wage, tips) are in [ontario-requirements.md](../06-canadian-regulations/ontario-requirements.md).

## The Canadian foodservice industry in numbers

From Restaurants Canada's summary of its 2024 Foodservice Facts report ([Charting a path to prosperity](https://www.restaurantscanada.org/restaurants-canada-charting-a-path-to-prosperity/)):

- Industry sales were forecast at almost $120 billion in 2024, up 4.9% from 2023, but real per-person spending fell 1.8%.
- Operators named food, insurance and utility costs as their biggest pressures.
- 45% of Canadians dine out at least once a week (49% of Millennials and Gen Z).
- The industry employs nearly 1.2 million people, about 6% of the workforce.
- Diners are more value-conscious; restaurants are responding with loyalty programs, limited-time offers and meal kits.

These are national figures from an industry association; Ontario-specific figures were not reviewed.

## Licensing and inspection rules that shape hospitality marketing

- **Liquor.** A liquor sales licence from the AGCO lets a business store, sell or serve liquor in places such as bars, restaurants and nightclubs; one-time events use special occasion permits, and delivery is a separate licence category ([AGCO: Liquor sales licence](https://www.agco.ca/en/alcohol/liquor-sales-licence)). **Unverified:** the AGCO's liquor advertising guidelines were not opened. Check them before promoting drink specials, free drinks or happy hours.
- **Food safety disclosure.** Toronto Public Health's [DineSafe](https://www.toronto.ca/community-people/health-wellness-care/health-programs-advice/food-safety/dinesafe/) program publishes inspection results (Pass, Conditional Pass, Closed) that the public can search by name or map. A conditional pass is publicly visible, so food safety is also a reputation issue.

## Business models and acquisition challenges in hospitality

| Model | Revenue drivers |
|---|---|
| Full-service restaurant / bar | Covers, average cheque, liquor margin, private events |
| Quick service / café | Volume, delivery, loyalty |
| Catering and events | Quotes, corporate accounts, seasonal peaks |
| Small hotel / B&B | Occupancy and rate; OTA dependence |

**Acquisition challenges (opinion):** delivery-app and OTA commissions eat margin; discovery happens on Google Maps, Instagram and review sites; private-event and catering enquiries are lost in email; value-conscious diners respond to offers (consistent with the Restaurants Canada findings above).

## Operational bottlenecks in restaurants and hospitality

**Opinion, based on common practice; not measured:** staff scheduling and turnover; menu changes not reflected on the website, Google and delivery apps; reservation no-shows; inventory and food cost tracking by hand; event quotes built manually.

## Website and digital presence features for hospitality

| Feature | Purpose |
|---|---|
| HTML menu (not a PDF) with prices, dietary and allergen notes | Search, mobile and accessibility |
| Reservation widget and private-event enquiry form | Direct bookings |
| Direct online ordering (commission-free) | Margin versus delivery apps (recommendation) |
| Accurate hours in Google Business Profile synced with site | Avoids lost visits |
| Gift cards and loyalty sign-up with CASL consent | Repeat visits |
| Events and specials page | Within AGCO advertising rules for liquor |

## Automation, AI and software in hospitality

StatCan found accommodation and food services had the lowest AI use of all sectors: 1.5% in Q2 2025 ([AI use, Q2 2025](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/11-621-m/11-621-m2025008-eng.htm)). Planned use over the next 12 months rose from 6.0% to 14.8% between Q3 2024 and Q3 2025 ([expected use of AI, Q3 2025](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/11-621-m/11-621-m2025011-eng.htm)).

**Automations (recommendations):** reservation reminders and no-show deposits; review and feedback request after visit; birthday and lapsed-guest emails; menu sync across website, Google and delivery apps; event enquiry to quote template.

**AI use cases (recommendations):** answering reservation, hours and allergen-policy questions (allergen answers must come from staff-verified data); drafting menu descriptions and social posts; summarising reviews to find recurring complaints; forecasting prep and staffing from sales history.

**Examples of software (not an endorsement; usage share not verified):** Toast, Square and Lightspeed POS; OpenTable, Resy and Tock reservations; 7shifts scheduling; Uber Eats, DoorDash and SkipTheDishes delivery.

**Custom development opportunities (recommendation):** private-event and catering quote builder; multi-location menu management feeding the website; loyalty integrations with the POS.
