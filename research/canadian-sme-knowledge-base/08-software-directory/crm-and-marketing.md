# CRM and Email Marketing Software for Canadian Small Businesses

This file profiles CRM (customer relationship management) and email marketing tools commonly used by Canadian SMEs: HubSpot, Zoho CRM and Bigin, and Mailchimp. Prices were read on each vendor's official pricing page on **2026-11-12**; vendors change prices and promotions often, so confirm before buying. CASL consent rules for email are in [../06-canadian-regulations/privacy-and-data-protection.md](../06-canadian-regulations/privacy-and-data-protection.md). Accounting, field service and office suites are in [operations-and-productivity.md](operations-and-productivity.md); automation tools in [ai-and-automation.md](ai-and-automation.md).

**Labels:** "Established" means a widely used product with a long market history; "Emerging" means newer or fast-changing. Capability, limitation and "best use" notes are **our assessment (opinion)** unless linked.

## HubSpot (CRM and marketing platform)

- **Official site:** [HubSpot Marketing Hub pricing](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/marketing) · **Status:** Established
- **Purpose:** All-in-one CRM with marketing email, forms, landing pages, ads tracking, sales pipeline and service tools.
- **Target customer:** Service businesses and B2B firms that want marketing and sales in one system, from solo to mid-size.
- **Public pricing (USD, read 2026-11-12):** Free for up to 2 users; Starter listed per seat (the page shows both $7 and $20/month per seat, so confirm the current figure); Professional from about $800 to $890/month with 3 core seats plus a one-time $3,000 onboarding fee; Enterprise from $3,600/month with 5 seats plus a $7,000 onboarding fee.
- **Integrations (opinion):** Large app marketplace; common links are Gmail/Outlook, QuickBooks, Shopify, Zoom and Zapier.
- **Limitations (opinion):** Cost jumps sharply from Starter to Professional; mandatory onboarding fees; pricing in USD exposes Canadian buyers to exchange rates.
- **Best use (recommendation):** A growing service firm that needs forms, email nurture and a sales pipeline on the free or Starter tier.
- **Alternatives:** Zoho CRM, Bigin, Pipedrive (not profiled), Mailchimp for email only.

## Zoho CRM and Bigin

- **Official sites:** [Zoho CRM pricing](https://www.zoho.com/crm/zohocrm-pricing.html) · Bigin by Zoho (bigin.com) · **Status:** Established
- **Purpose:** Zoho CRM is a configurable sales CRM; Bigin is Zoho's simpler pipeline CRM for very small teams.
- **Target customer:** Price-sensitive SMEs; businesses already using other Zoho apps (Books, Mail, Desk).
- **Public pricing (read 2026-11-12):** Zoho CRM editions are Free, Standard, Professional, Enterprise and Ultimate. The Free edition is "free forever for 3 users"; annual plans save up to 34%; Zoho CRM Plus "starts at $20/user/month" ([Zoho CRM pricing](https://www.zoho.com/crm/zohocrm-pricing.html)). **Unverified:** per-user prices for paid CRM editions and Bigin did not render on the pages fetched; the currency shown depends on the visitor's region.
- **Integrations (opinion):** Deep links across the Zoho suite; Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 and Zapier.
- **Limitations (opinion):** Configuration takes time; interfaces vary across Zoho apps.
- **Best use (recommendation):** A small team that wants a low-cost CRM with room to customize, or Bigin for a simple deal pipeline.
- **Alternatives:** HubSpot, Pipedrive (not profiled).

## Mailchimp (email marketing)

- **Official site:** [Mailchimp pricing (Canada)](https://mailchimp.com/en-ca/pricing/marketing/) · **Status:** Established
- **Purpose:** Email newsletters, automations, sign-up forms and basic landing pages.
- **Target customer:** Retailers, restaurants, clinics and service firms sending newsletters and promotions.
- **Public pricing (read 2026-11-12; page appears to show USD):** Free: up to 250 contacts and 500 emails/month (250/day). Essentials from $13/month, Standard from $20/month, Premium from $350/month, all shown at 50% off for the first 12 months; the post-promotion price was not shown. Send limits are multiples of the contact tier.
- **Integrations (opinion):** Shopify (via apps), Square, WooCommerce, Zapier, many form tools.
- **Limitations (opinion):** Price rises with contact count; you pay for unsubscribed/inactive contacts unless cleaned; promo pricing makes year-two cost unclear.
- **Best use (recommendation):** A business with a modest list that wants simple, CASL-compliant newsletters (footer and unsubscribe added automatically).
- **Alternatives:** HubSpot marketing email, Zoho Campaigns, Shopify Email (for Shopify stores, not profiled).

## Choosing a CRM or email tool: quick comparison

| Need | Start with (recommendation) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Free CRM, 1 to 2 users | HubSpot Free | Free tier for 2 users with forms and pipeline |
| Free CRM, 3 users | Zoho CRM Free | Free for 3 users |
| Newsletter only, small list | Mailchimp Free | 250 contacts free |
| Marketing automation at scale | HubSpot Professional | Full automation, but from ~US$800/month plus onboarding |

**Recommendation:** Whatever the tool, record how and when each contact gave consent, because CASL places the burden of proof on the sender.
