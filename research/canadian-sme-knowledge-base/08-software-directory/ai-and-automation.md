# AI Assistants and Automation Tools for Canadian Small Businesses

This file profiles AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot) and workflow automation tools (Zapier, Make, n8n) used by Canadian SMEs. Prices were read on official pages on **2026-11-12** and are in **USD** unless stated; AI pricing changes frequently. Privacy duties when sending customer data to these tools are in [../06-canadian-regulations/privacy-and-data-protection.md](../06-canadian-regulations/privacy-and-data-protection.md). Office suites that bundle AI are in [operations-and-productivity.md](operations-and-productivity.md).

**Labels:** Capability, limitation and "best use" notes are **our assessment (opinion)** unless linked. All AI assistants here are labelled **Emerging**: established vendors, but plans and features change every few months.

## ChatGPT (OpenAI)

- **Official site:** [ChatGPT Business overview](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8792828-chatgpt-business-overview) · **Status:** Emerging
- **Purpose:** General AI assistant for writing, analysis, research, image generation and custom GPTs.
- **Public pricing:** **Unverified.** A search summary of OpenAI's help page gave ChatGPT Business prices of USD $20/user/month billed annually or $25 monthly (Standard seat), minimum 2 seats, but the page itself returned 403 on 2026-11-12, so check OpenAI's site before quoting. Plus and Go prices are also unverified (OpenAI's consumer pricing page blocked automated access).
- **Limitations (opinion):** Can produce confident errors; staff need rules on what customer data may be entered.
- **Best use (recommendation):** A team workspace (Business plan) rather than personal accounts, so company data stays under company admin control.
- **Alternatives:** Claude, Gemini, Copilot.

## Claude (Anthropic)

- **Official site:** [Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing) · **Status:** Emerging
- **Public pricing (read 2026-11-12, excluding tax):** Free; Pro $17/month billed annually ($200/year) or $20 monthly; Max $100/month; Ultra $200/month. Team (2 to 150 people): Standard seat $20/seat/month annually or $25 monthly; Premium seat $100 annually or $125 monthly. Enterprise $20/seat/month annually, 20-seat minimum, plus usage at API rates.
- **Best use (recommendation):** Long documents, proposals, policy drafting and coding help.
- **Alternatives:** ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot.

## Gemini (Google) and Microsoft Copilot

- **Gemini:** included in Google Workspace business plans; Starter includes Gemini in Gmail with limited chat, Standard adds Gemini in Docs, Meet and more ([Google Workspace pricing](https://workspace.google.com/intl/en_ca/pricing.html), from US$7/user/month). **Best use (recommendation):** firms already on Workspace.
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot:** AI in Word, Excel, Outlook and Teams. **Unverified:** Canadian prices; search results on 2026-11-12 showed conflicting CAD figures across Microsoft pages (for example Copilot Business around $24 to $28.50/user/month), and the pages could not be opened. Confirm on microsoft.com/en-ca before budgeting. **Best use (recommendation):** firms already on Microsoft 365.

## Zapier (no-code automation)

- **Official site:** [Zapier pricing](https://zapier.com/pricing) · **Status:** Established
- **Purpose:** Connects thousands of apps with trigger-action "Zaps" (for example web form to CRM to Slack).
- **Public pricing (USD, annual billing, read 2026-11-12):** Free 100 tasks/month, 2-step Zaps; Pro from $19.99/month (750 tasks), $49 (2,000), $129 (10,000); Team from $69/month (2,000 tasks, up to 25 seats). Monthly billing costs about 50% more; non-profits get 15% off; 14-day Pro trial.
- **Limitations (opinion):** Costs scale with task volume; complex logic gets expensive.
- **Best use (recommendation):** Non-technical owners automating lead capture and notifications.
- **Alternatives:** Make, n8n.

## Make (visual automation)

- **Official site:** [Make pricing](https://www.make.com/en/pricing) · **Status:** Established
- **Public pricing (read 2026-11-12):** Free plan with 1,000 credits/month; paid Core, Pro, Teams and Enterprise plans billed by credits; a Start-up offer gives 50% off for organizations under 20 employees. **Unverified:** paid plan prices did not render on the page.
- **Best use (recommendation):** Multi-step workflows with branching at lower cost per step than Zapier (opinion).
- **Alternatives:** Zapier, n8n.

## n8n (open-source workflow automation)

- **Official site:** [n8n pricing](https://n8n.io/pricing/) · **Status:** Emerging
- **Public pricing (EUR, billed annually, read 2026-11-12):** Starter €20/month (2,500 executions); Pro €50/month (10,000 executions); Business €667/month (self-hosted); Enterprise custom. All plans include unlimited users and workflows; billing counts whole workflow runs, not steps. A self-hosted Community edition is on GitHub (**Unverified** whether free of charge for all uses).
- **Limitations (opinion):** Self-hosting needs technical skill and maintenance.
- **Best use (recommendation):** AI agent workflows and high-volume automations, or when data must stay on Canadian-hosted servers.
- **Alternatives:** Make, Zapier.
