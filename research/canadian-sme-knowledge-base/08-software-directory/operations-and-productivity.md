# Accounting, Field Service, Clinic and Office Software for Canadian Small Businesses

This file profiles operations software Canadian SMEs commonly use: accounting (QuickBooks Online, Xero, Wave), field service (Jobber), clinic management (Jane App) and office suites (Microsoft 365, Google Workspace). Prices were read on official pricing pages on **2026-11-12**; promotions change often. CRA record-keeping rules for cloud accounting are in [../06-canadian-regulations/federal-requirements.md](../06-canadian-regulations/federal-requirements.md). Payments are in [ecommerce-and-payments.md](ecommerce-and-payments.md); AI features in [ai-and-automation.md](ai-and-automation.md).

**Labels:** Capability, limitation and "best use" notes are **our assessment (opinion)** unless linked.

## QuickBooks Online Canada (accounting)

- **Official site:** [QuickBooks Canada pricing](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/ca/pricing/) · **Status:** Established
- **Purpose:** Bookkeeping, invoicing, GST/HST tracking, bank feeds, payroll add-on.
- **Target customer:** Most small businesses and their bookkeepers/accountants.

| Plan (CAD/month, read 2026-11-12) | Regular | Promo shown | Users |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lite | $20 | $2 | 1 |
| EasyStart | $30 | $3 | 1 |
| Plus | $110 | $11 | up to 5 |
| Advanced | $220 | $22 | up to 25 |

- Payroll add-on: Core $30 + $5/employee; Premium $55 + $8/employee; Elite $80 + $15/employee (regular prices). Lite does not support payroll.
- **Limitations (opinion):** Regular prices have risen; feature gating pushes growing firms to Plus.
- **Best use (recommendation):** Default choice when your accountant uses QuickBooks.
- **Alternatives:** Xero, Wave.

## Xero (accounting)

- **Official site:** [Xero Canada pricing](https://www.xero.com/ca/pricing-plans/) · **Status:** Established
- **Public pricing (CAD/month, read 2026-11-12):** Starter $25 (20 invoices and 5 bills per month); Standard $60; Premium $80. New customers get 80% off for 3 months.
- **Limitations (opinion):** Canadian payroll is not built in; Starter limits are tight.
- **Best use (recommendation):** Firms whose accountant works in Xero, or that want unlimited users.
- **Alternatives:** QuickBooks Online, Wave.

## Wave (accounting and invoicing)

- **Official site:** [Wave pricing](https://www.waveapps.com/pricing) · **Status:** Established
- **Public pricing (CAD, read 2026-11-12):** Starter $0 (unlimited invoices, estimates, bills, bookkeeping); Pro $25/month or $250/year. Card processing in Canada: 2.9% + $0.60 (Amex 3.4% + $0.60) on Starter; Pro waives the $0.60 on the first 10 transactions per month.
- **Unverified:** Whether Wave payroll is available in Canada was not stated on the pricing page.
- **Best use (recommendation):** Sole proprietors and freelancers who mainly send invoices.
- **Alternatives:** QuickBooks EasyStart, Xero Starter.

## Jobber (field service management)

- **Official site:** [Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/) · **Status:** Established
- **Purpose:** Quotes, scheduling, dispatch, invoicing and payments for home-service trades.
- **Target customer:** Landscaping, cleaning, HVAC, plumbing, renovation and similar crews.
- **Public pricing (USD/month, read 2026-11-12):** Core (1 user) $29 billed annually / $49 no commitment; Connect (1 user) $99 / $139; Grow (1 user) $149 / $199; Plus (5 users) $399 / $499. Extra users $29/month each.
- **Best use (recommendation):** Trades businesses replacing paper quotes and whiteboard scheduling.
- **Alternatives:** Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan (not profiled).

## Jane App (clinic management)

- **Official site:** [Jane App pricing](https://jane.app/pricing) · **Status:** Established
- **Purpose:** Online booking, charting, billing, telehealth for health and wellness clinics.
- **Public pricing (CAD/month, read 2026-11-12):** Balance $59 (1 practitioner, 20 appointments/month); Practice $79; Thrive $99 (each with 1 full-time practitioner; additional practitioners billed by booked hours). Add-ons: AI Scribe $15/practitioner; Insurance Billing $20 + per practitioner; Jane Payroll $40 + $6/staff; Websites $59/clinic.
- **Best use (recommendation):** Physio, chiro, massage, counselling clinics; check PHIPA duties in [../06-canadian-regulations/privacy-and-data-protection.md](../06-canadian-regulations/privacy-and-data-protection.md).

## Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace (office suites)

- **Official sites:** Microsoft 365 business plans (microsoft.com/en-ca) · [Google Workspace pricing](https://workspace.google.com/intl/en_ca/pricing.html) · **Status:** Established

| Suite | Plans | Public pricing (read 2026-11-12) |
|---|---|---|
| Google Workspace (USD/user/month, annual) | Starter / Standard / Plus | $7 / $14 / $22; storage 30 GB / 2 TB / 5 TB pooled; Gemini included; max 300 users |
| Microsoft 365 (CAD) | Basic / Standard / Premium | **Unverified:** the Microsoft page timed out; search snippets showed Business Standard with Copilot at $31.90/user/month (yearly), but figures conflicted across Microsoft pages |

- **Best use (recommendation):** Choose Microsoft 365 if staff live in Excel/Word/Outlook desktop apps; Google Workspace for browser-first teams.
