# Ecommerce Platforms and Payment Processors for Canadian Small Businesses

This file profiles the online store and payment tools Canadian SMEs commonly use: Shopify, Square, Stripe, Moneris and Interac. Prices were read on official pricing pages on **2026-11-12** and are in **CAD** unless stated; rates change, so confirm before signing. Invoicing payments in accounting tools (QuickBooks, Wave) are in [operations-and-productivity.md](operations-and-productivity.md). Ontario consumer protection rules for online sales are in [../06-canadian-regulations/ontario-requirements.md](../06-canadian-regulations/ontario-requirements.md).

**Labels:** Capability, limitation and "best use" notes are **our assessment (opinion)** unless linked. "Established" = long market history.

## Shopify (online store and point of sale)

- **Official site:** [Shopify Canada pricing](https://www.shopify.com/ca/pricing) · **Status:** Established
- **Purpose:** Hosted online store, checkout, payments (Shopify Payments), inventory and in-store POS.
- **Target customer:** Retailers and brands selling products online, with or without a physical store.

| Plan (CAD, read 2026-11-12) | Yearly billing /month | Monthly billing | Online card rate | In-person card | In-person Interac | Third-party payment fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $37 | $49 | 2.8% + 30¢ | 2.6% | 15¢ | 2% |
| Grow | $99 | $132 | 2.6% + 30¢ | 2.5% | 15¢ | 1% |
| Advanced | $389 | $517 | 2.4% + 30¢ | 2.4% | 15¢ | 0.6% |
| Plus | from $3,400 | n/a | 2.15% + 30¢ | 2.4% | 10¢ | 0.2% |

- POS Pro add-on: $119 per location per month.
- **Limitations (opinion):** App subscriptions add up; using a payment processor other than Shopify Payments adds the third-party fee above.
- **Best use (recommendation):** Product sellers who want a reliable store without custom development.
- **Alternatives:** Square Online, WooCommerce (not profiled), custom build.

## Square (point of sale and payments)

- **Official site:** [Square Canada pricing](https://squareup.com/ca/en/pricing) · **Status:** Established
- **Purpose:** Card readers and POS software, online checkout, invoices, appointments.
- **Target customer:** Cafés, restaurants, retail, salons and appointment businesses.
- **Public pricing (CAD, read 2026-11-12):** In-person credit 2.5% (cards issued outside Canada +1.5%); in-person debit (tap/insert) 0.75% + 7¢; online 2.8% + 30¢; keyed-in or card-on-file 3.3% + 15¢. Software plans: Free; Plus from $35/month; Premium custom.
- **Limitations (opinion):** Flat rates are simple but can cost more than negotiated rates at high volume.
- **Best use (recommendation):** A small in-person business that wants to start taking cards the same week.
- **Alternatives:** Shopify POS, Moneris, Clover (not profiled).

## Stripe (online payments infrastructure)

- **Official site:** [Stripe Canada pricing](https://stripe.com/en-ca/pricing) · **Status:** Established
- **Purpose:** Developer-focused payments for websites, apps, subscriptions and invoices.
- **Target customer:** Businesses with a custom website or app, SaaS and subscription businesses.
- **Public pricing (CAD, read 2026-11-12):** Domestic cards 2.9% + $0.30; international cards +0.8%; currency conversion +2%; manually entered cards +0.5%. Pre-authorized debits 1% + $0.40 (capped at $5). In person (Terminal): 2.7% + $0.05; Interac debit $0.15.
- **Limitations (opinion):** Needs a developer or a platform integration; support is mostly online.
- **Best use (recommendation):** Custom sites, booking systems and recurring billing.
- **Alternatives:** Square, PayPal (not profiled), Shopify Payments for Shopify stores.

## Moneris (Canadian merchant acquirer)

- **Official site:** moneris.com · **Status:** Established Canadian processor (ownership and company details **Unverified**; site not reachable on 2026-11-12)
- **Purpose:** Card and Interac debit terminals, online gateway and POS for Canadian merchants.
- **Target customer:** Retailers, restaurants and higher-volume merchants that want a Canadian acquirer and negotiated rates.
- **Public pricing:** **Unverified.** The Moneris website blocked automated access on 2026-11-12; rates are commonly quoted per merchant. Ask for an itemized quote (per-transaction rates, terminal rental, monthly and cancellation fees).
- **Best use (recommendation):** Established businesses with enough card volume to negotiate.
- **Alternatives:** Square, Stripe, Shopify Payments.

## Interac (debit and e-Transfer for business)

- **Official site:** [Interac for business](https://www.interac.ca/en/business/) · **Status:** Established
- **What it offers businesses:** Interac e-Transfer for Business (send and receive payments, including Request Money), Interac Debit acceptance at checkout, Interac Direct for online bank payments, and Interac verification services.
- **Access:** Through your bank or a payment acquirer; the page links to "Find an Acquirer". Debit acceptance is priced by your processor (for example 15¢ in person on Shopify and Stripe, above).
- **Pricing:** Not listed on the Interac business page.
- **Best use (recommendation):** Low-cost debit acceptance in person, and e-Transfer for B2B invoices where card fees would be high.
