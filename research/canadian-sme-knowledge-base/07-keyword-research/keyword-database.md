# Keyword Database for Canadian and Toronto Small Business Searches

## Data gap: no search volume or competition data

**No SEO tool data was available for this research.** Every row in this file therefore shows **Not measured** for search volume and competition. Do not read the order of keywords as a ranking of demand, and do not quote any keyword here as "most searched".

Two kinds of evidence are used, and each row says which:

- **Autocomplete (observed 2026-11-12):** the phrase appeared as a Google autocomplete suggestion when we queried Google's public suggestion endpoint with Canadian settings (`gl=ca`, `hl=en`). Autocomplete shows that a phrase is searched often enough to be suggested; it does **not** give volume, trend or difficulty. Results vary by time, location and personalization.
- **Researcher-derived from owner questions:** the phrase was written by the researcher from a question in [question-database.md](question-database.md). It is a plausible search phrasing, not an observed query.

Google Trends and "People also ask" were not observed for this file.

**Tools that would fill the gap (Recommendation):**

| Tool | What it adds | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Google Keyword Planner | Monthly volume ranges and paid competition by location (Canada, Ontario, Toronto) | Free with a Google Ads account |
| Google Search Console | Real queries, impressions and clicks for a site that already ranks | Free, needs a verified site |
| Ahrefs or Semrush | Volume estimates, keyword difficulty, competitor rankings, "questions" reports | Paid subscription |
| Google Trends | Relative interest over time and by province (relative index, not volume) | Free |
